why we exist

Our Mission

We founded lifesports4all because we believe sport should be for everyone—not only kids who already have access to coaches, equipment, and programs.

Every child deserves the chance to learn, compete, and feel proud of what they can do. We run welcoming, hands-on sessions in tennis, ping pong, and golf where underprivileged youth build real skills, grow in confidence, and carry lessons from the court into school, friendships, and everyday life.

  • Free, volunteer-led instruction focused on fundamentals and fun
  • A supportive environment where effort and improvement matter
  • Mentorship that helps kids build habits beyond sports

about

Meet the people behind lifesports4all.

Nikhil Anand

Co-Founder

Nikhil Anand is a dedicated student-athlete and mentor with a strong passion for giving back through sports. As a recruitable Division I athlete with a perfect GPA, he understands the discipline, consistency, and mindset it takes to develop both on and off the court. He's committed to sharing the lessons he's learned through athletics with kids in his community, helping them build confidence, character, and lifelong habits through sport. Nikhil is grateful for the opportunity to co-found lifesports4all and create a positive environment where kids can learn, grow, and feel supported.

Rainer Schmitz

Co-Founder

Rainer Schmitz is a dedicated teacher and learner of life sports. With a strong athletic background of being a 4 star athlete with multiple D1 scholarship opportunities, he understands what it takes to master a sport. He is committed to passing on his knowledge and learning from the kids within his community. Throughout his high school career, on top of maintaining a 4.0 GPA, he has played in tournaments including the Pan-American Championships and won many national level tournaments. He finds joy in teaching and learning from others and is grateful to have the opportunity to found this non-profit.

our commitment

our promise

To every kid—and every family—we serve, our promise is straightforward:

  • We show up. With our skills, our time, and the experience we've gained as athletes and mentors.
  • We lead with patience. Encouraging effort, celebrating progress, and helping kids believe in themselves.
  • We build a safe space. Where every child feels respected, supported, and motivated to keep improving.

Sport is how we connect—but character, confidence, and consistency are what we hope every child takes home.
